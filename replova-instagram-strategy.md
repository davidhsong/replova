## 2. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — "The Numbers Don't Lie" (Education + Stats)
**Purpose:** Position Replova as the credibility authority for med spa reputation. Educate owners on why reviews = revenue. Build trust before asking for anything.
**Frequency:** 3× per week (Mon, Wed, Fri)

**Example post ideas:**
1. "The average med spa owner replies to 1 in 8 Google reviews. Here's what the 7 ignored ones cost you." (carousel: stats on conversion impact)
2. "A half-star rating increase on Google = 5–9% revenue lift. Here's how that math hits a $500K clinic." (single image with stat overlay)
3. "57% of patients read 4+ reviews before booking an aesthetic appointment. What are yours saying?" (poll story → feed post)

---

### Pillar 2 — "Before Replova / After Replova" (Social Proof + Transformation)
**Purpose:** Let real customer outcomes do the selling. Showcase dramatic, specific before/after metrics — not feelings, numbers.
**Frequency:** 2× per week (Tue, Sat)

**Example post ideas:**
1. Dr. Amanda Chen story: 47 unanswered reviews → 100% response rate in one week. (quote card + stat)
2. Kayla Morrison story: discovered hidden wait-time problem through sentiment analysis → +0.5 stars in 60 days. (carousel walkthrough)
3. Marcus Webb: one employee's entire job eliminated — 6 locations now handled automatically every morning. (reel talking head format)

---

### Pillar 3 — "Inside the Product" (Feature Showcase)
**Purpose:** Demystify what Replova actually does. Show the dashboard, the AI drafts, the competitor view. Make prospects feel familiar before they sign up.
**Frequency:** 2× per week (Wed, Sun)

**Example post ideas:**
1. Screen recording of three AI reply drafts (Professional, Warm, Brief) generated for the same review — 15-second reel.
2. Carousel: "What your Replova dashboard tells you every morning" — score, sentiment trend, staff mentions, unanswered count.
3. Competitor radar feature walkthrough: "You're at 4.3. The spa two blocks away is at 4.7. Here's what Replova shows you."

---

### Pillar 4 — "Med Spa Business Mindset" (Industry Value + Thought Leadership)
**Purpose:** Serve the audience even when not pitching. Build a community of sharp aesthetic business operators. Earn the follow.
**Frequency:** 2× per week (Thu, Sun)

**Example post ideas:**
1. "5 things med spa owners should audit every Monday morning" (carousel — reviews is #1, of course)
2. "Why negative reviews are the most valuable data your clinic generates" (reframe education post)
3. "What HydraFacial clients say vs. what Botox clients say — and how that should change your marketing" (treatment sentiment data storytelling)

---

### Pillar 5 — "Behind the Build" (Brand + Founder Authenticity)
**Purpose:** Make Replova feel like a real company built by people who care about this industry, not a faceless SaaS. Build connection and trust with early adopters.
**Frequency:** 1× per week (Fri or Sat)

## 5. Ten Reel / Video Concepts

---

**Reel 1 — "The 47 Reviews Problem"**
- **Hook (first 3 sec):** Text on screen: "She had 47 unanswered reviews." Cut to mock dashboard showing 47 reviews, 0 replies.
- **Script outline:** VO: "This is a real clinic. A real owner. 47 Google reviews sitting there with zero replies. We connected Replova on a Monday. By Friday, every single one had a personalized response. Response rate: 12% → 100%." Show the review list filling with green checkmarks.
- **On-screen text:** "47 → 0 unanswered" / "One week." / "Powered by Replova"
- **Format:** 15–30 sec. Text-driven, no talking head needed. Lo-fi screen recording style.

---

**Reel 2 — "Three Replies, One Review"**
- **Hook (first 3 sec):** "What if every Google review came with 3 pre-written replies?" Show a 5-star review landing.
- **Script outline:** Show the same 5-star review with three reply drafts appearing below it — Professional, Warm, Brief. User selects "Warm," edits one word, taps "Post to Google." Done in 12 seconds.
- **On-screen text:** "Professional." / "Warm." / "Brief." / "Your choice. One tap."
- **Format:** 20 sec. Screen recording with minimal VO or music only.
